# Recommend Sellsy to your contacts and be rewarded


Whether you are an administrator (account manager) or a user, you have everything to gain

## You are responsible for the Sellsy account


## 1. Introduce Sellsy to your contacts

Go to your referral space in your Sellsy account. Share your unique referral link with your contacts or invite them to discover the Sellsy solution by sending them a personalized email. Don't have the time? We'll take care of it. Give your contacts to your account manager or send them directly to us by email. They will be automatically linked to your account.


## 2. Your contacts subscribe to Sellsy

Your contacts will be accompanied by our advisors: Welcome call, discovery webinar, help with account setup: all Sellsy support is 100% behind them. Your referrals will be visible on your dashboard in your referral space.


## 3. Receive your commission

You are rewarded! You will receive a 20% commission, calculated on the amount of the 1st annual subscription of your referral. The commission is paid to you in cash, by check or bank transfer: an excellent way to make your Sellsy subscription profitable. The commission is limited to 5000 € per account. 


## You are a Sellsy user

## 1. Share Sellsy with your contacts

Give your contacts to your account manager, or send them to us directly by email. They will automatically be linked to your account. 

## 2. Your contacts subscribe to Sellsy

Your contacts will be accompanied by our advisors: welcome call, discovery webinar, help with account settings: all Sellsy support is 100% dedicated to them. 

## 3. Receive your commission

You are rewarded! Google Home Mini, iPad Pro, Headset... You choose from our gift catalog. Your company will also receive a 20% commission, calculated on the amount of the 1st annual subscription of your sponsored contact.
