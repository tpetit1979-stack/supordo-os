# Who are we? 


## The CRM suite for small businesses and SMES


Sellsy is a French company based in La Rochelle and Bordeaux that offers a complete CRM suite for small and medium-sized businesses, integrating marketing, sales, billing, payment and cash management tools.

Since its creation in 2009, Sellsy has placed innovation at the heart of its strategy in order to offer companies and their employees simple, effective and secure digital solutions.

Today, its solution enables more than 48,000 users in 19,000 companies to save time, boost their sales and secure their income.

## The Sellsy spirit

Since the beginning of the Sellsy adventure, our teams have been at the heart of our strategy, both in terms of product development and company dynamics.

Sellsy is a team of 220 employees who work hand in hand to innovate and help companies grow, all with accessibility and kindness.

For several years now, the company's managers have been developing the solution and uniting a team of talented people around an ambitious project: to become the European CRM suite that meets the requirements of all SMEs.
